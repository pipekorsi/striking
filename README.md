# Striking

An interactive map of the real places Cormoran Strike and Robin Ellacott visit in the Strike novels (as written by Robert Galbraith), from *The Cuckoo's Calling* to *The Hallmarked Man*.

*Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the author or publishers.*

## What's on the map
- **375 places** across all eight books: pubs, streets, homes, hotels, offices and further afield (Cornwall, Norfolk, Sark and elsewhere).
- Only places a main character **actually visits** are included, not places merely mentioned. Fictional venues are left out, but the real street they sit on is kept (and flagged).
- Each pin shows who visited (Strike, Robin or both), the books and chapters, and a short note.
- Filter by who visited, book, type of place, town, London area, how exact the pin is, and more. A "where am I" button and a nearest-first list make it handy for sightseeing.

Some pins are approximate (street, area or town level) and are drawn differently.

## How it's built
A static page (`index.html` + `data.js`) using [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) and OpenStreetMap tiles. No build step or backend.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
